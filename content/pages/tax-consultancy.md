## 2. Signs you need tax support
Status: approved

Eyebrow: Sound familiar?
Heading: When to Talk to a Tax Adviser
Text: Tax problems rarely announce themselves. They build up quietly until a deadline, a penalty or a letter from the tax authority brings them to light. If any of these sound familiar, it is time for expert support.
- Deadlines that creep up — Returns are filed late or in a rush, and penalties keep appearing.
- Unsure what you owe — You are not certain which taxes apply to you, or whether you are paying too much or too little.
- Growth that changes the picture — New staff, new locations, new products or new investors bring new tax obligations.
- A letter from the tax authority — An assessment, audit notice or enquiry has arrived and you need to respond well.
- Changing rules — Tax laws and rates change, and you need to know what that means for your organisation.
- Big decisions ahead — A restructure, acquisition, new contract or investment has tax consequences you want to understand first.
Text: Getting advice early costs far less than fixing problems after the fact.
