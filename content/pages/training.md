## 5. The learning journey
Status: approved

Eyebrow: Our approach
Heading: A Learning Journey, Not Just a Training Day
Text: Real learning happens before, during and after the training session. That is why every JoDyn programme follows a structured learning journey.

### Before Training
Text: We clarify learning objectives with you, understand participants' roles and challenges, and, where needed, conduct a short pre-training assessment. This ensures the programme is relevant from the first minute.

### During Training
Text: Participants learn through a balance of short expert input, real case studies, group discussions, role plays and practical exercises. Every session ends with participants applying the learning to their own work.

### After Training
Text: Each participant leaves with a personal action plan. We gather feedback, share a programme report with your organisation and remain available to support the application of learning back at work.
What you receive:
- Certificate of Participation — Issued to every participant who completes the programme.
- Course Materials — Workbooks, slides and resources to refer back to after the programme.
- Practical Tools — Templates, checklists and frameworks to use at work.
- Personal Action Plan — A clear plan for applying the learning in your role.
- Post-Training Support — Access to the facilitator for follow-up questions after the programme.
- Programme Report (for organisations) — A summary of attendance, feedback and recommendations for in-house programmes.
