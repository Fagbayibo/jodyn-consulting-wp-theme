### 1. What Cookies Are
Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They let the website work properly, remember your choices and, in some cases, understand how visitors use it. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work in much the same way, and this policy covers them too.

### 2. The Cookies We Use
We keep cookies to a minimum.

**Essential cookies**
These are needed for the website to work and are always on.
- jodyn_consent: remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies, so we do not ask again on every page. Kept for 12 months.
- WordPress sign-in cookies: set only when our staff sign in to manage the site. Visitors who browse the site, register for a programme or send us a message do not receive them.

**Analytics cookies**
With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand which pages are visited, how long visitors stay and how they found us, so we can improve the site. Google Analytics collects information such as the pages you visit, your browser and device type and your approximate location. It does not tell us who you are.
- _ga: distinguishes one visitor from another. Kept for 2 years.
- _ga_[ID]: keeps track of your visit. Kept for 2 years.

These cookies are only set if you choose Accept in our cookie banner. If you choose Essential only, Google Analytics does not load at all.

### 3. Third-Party Services
Our website loads its font from Google Fonts. To deliver the font, Google receives your IP address and browser details. Google Fonts does not set cookies. You can read how Google handles this information in Google's privacy policy.

Links to our social media pages take you to those platforms, which have their own cookie policies.

### 4. How to Control Cookies
You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw your consent, Google Analytics stops loading from your next page view.

You can also view, block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block cookies from particular websites or from third parties. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the website from working.

### 5. Changes to This Policy
