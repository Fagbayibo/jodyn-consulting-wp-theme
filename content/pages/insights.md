## Article template (every article)
Status: approved

Top to bottom:
- Breadcrumb: Home › Insights › [Category]
- Header: category tag, title (H1), author name, date and reading time
- Featured image, full width, with alt text
- Article body: introduction, H2 sections, H3 sub-sections where needed
- Mid-article call to action: a highlighted box halfway through, linking to a related programme or service (added by the writer in the editor)
- Key takeaways: a summary box near the end (added by the writer in the editor)
- Author box: photo, name, title and a one-line bio (from the author's WordPress profile)
- Share buttons: LinkedIn, WhatsApp, X and email
- Related articles: three cards from the same category
