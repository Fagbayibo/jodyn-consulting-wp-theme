### Advisory

- How long does an advisory engagement take? — It depends on the scope. A focused workshop or strategy retreat can be completed in one to three days, while a full strategy, restructuring or HR systems project typically runs over several weeks or months. We agree on timelines with you before any work begins.
- How much do your advisory services cost? — Every engagement is scoped to your needs, so fees depend on the size, complexity and duration of the work. After an initial conversation, we provide a clear proposal with the scope, deliverables, timeline and fees.
- Can advisory sessions be held online? — Yes. Many parts of our engagements, including interviews, workshops and progress reviews, can be delivered online. We also offer in-person and hybrid options depending on your preference.

### Systems Audit

- Is a systems audit the same as a statutory financial audit? — No. A statutory audit gives an opinion on your financial statements. A systems audit looks at how your processes, controls and systems work, so you can reduce risk and run more efficiently. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm whether JoDyn also offers statutory financial audits.]
- How long does a systems audit take? — It depends on the scope and the size of your organisation. A focused review of one area can take one to two weeks, while a wider review takes several weeks. We agree the timeline before any work begins.
- How much does an audit cost? — Fees depend on the scope, size and complexity of the review. After an initial conversation, we send a clear proposal with the scope, deliverables, timeline and fees.
- Will the audit disrupt our work? — We plan interviews and testing around your team's schedule and keep requests to what the review needs.
- Can you help us fix the issues you find? — Yes. Our advisory and training teams can help you put the recommendations in place, or you can implement them yourselves using the action plan.
