SECTION 2 ·  CHALLENGES WE SOLVE
[Label]  SOUND FAMILIAR?
[H2]  Challenges We Help Organisations Overcome
Every organisation reaches a point where what got it here will not get it to the next level. If any of these sound familiar, our advisory team can help.
Strategy without execution: Plans are written and approved, but progress stalls and priorities keep shifting.
Growth that outpaces structure: The business has grown, but roles, processes and systems have not kept up.
Underperforming teams: Targets are missed, accountability is unclear and productivity is lower than it should be.
A leadership gap: Managers are capable in their roles but not yet equipped to lead people and drive results.
Resistance to change: New initiatives are introduced, but the culture and behaviours stay the same.
Low engagement and high turnover: Good people are leaving, and those who stay are not fully committed.
These challenges are common, and they are solvable. The right diagnosis, a clear plan and the support to see it through make all the difference.

SECTION 4 ·  HOW AN ENGAGEMENT WORKS
[Label]  OUR APPROACH
[H2]  How We Work With You
Every advisory engagement follows a clear, structured process. You will always know what we are doing, why it matters and what you will receive at each stage.
Understand: We begin with conversations, interviews and a review of relevant information to understand your goals, challenges and context. You receive: a clear summary of the problem and agreed objectives.
Design: We analyse our findings and develop practical recommendations tailored to your organisation. You receive a findings report and a recommended solution or roadmap.
Deliver: We support implementation alongside your team through workshops, tools, coaching and hands-on guidance. You receive the frameworks, templates and support needed to put the solution into action.
Measure & Sustain: We review progress against agreed measures and recommend next steps to keep the momentum going. You receive: a progress review and a plan for sustaining results.

SECTION 8 ·  FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
[Label]  FAQS
[H2]  Frequently Asked Questions
How long does an advisory engagement take?
It depends on the scope. A focused workshop or strategy retreat can be completed in one to three days, while a full strategy, restructuring or HR systems project typically runs over several weeks or months. We agree on timelines with you before any work begins.
How much do your advisory services cost?
Every engagement is scoped to your needs, so fees depend on the size, complexity and duration of the work. After an initial conversation, we provide a clear proposal with the scope, deliverables, timeline and fees.
Do you work with small and growing businesses?
Yes. Our advisory services are designed for organisations of all sizes, from SMEs putting structures in place to larger organisations and public institutions. Our solutions are scaled to fit your stage and resources.
Can advisory sessions be held online?
Yes. Many parts of our engagements, including interviews, workshops and progress reviews, can be delivered online. We also offer in-person and hybrid options depending on your preference.
Will our information be kept confidential?
Absolutely. All information you share with us is treated with strict confidentiality and used only for the purpose of the engagement. We are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement where required.
How do we get started?
Simply book a consultation or send us a message. We will schedule a conversation to understand your needs, then recommend the right approach and next steps.
